    0%

    Moves of three semitones or more shift only the sung tone and carry the breath and air across separately. Some voices sound more natural this way, others pick up a faint trace of the original pitch; compare by ear with B.

    At 0% the Draw tool follows your line exactly. Raise it to let some of your own tiny pitch movement ride on top of a drawn line, which helps long held lines sound less synthetic.

    Untouched audio is passed through sample-for-sample. Edited notes are rebuilt one glottal cycle at a time, so the formants, breath and consonants of your voice stay where they were. When a note moves, the consonant just before it moves with it; only the gaps between notes are stretched. On big upward moves (around six semitones and more), the breath that the shift would turn into buzz is measured and put back.

    Noise reduction

    You hear this with Hear it polished, which switches on when you move a control.

    Off

    Learns the hiss, fan or room noise from the quietest parts of your recording, then takes it out of the whole take. Start around 40–60%; higher values remove more but can thin out quiet breaths.

    Hum removal

    You hear this with Hear it polished, which switches on when you move a control.

    Narrow filters on mains hum and its overtones. In the UK and Europe the mains is 50 Hz; in the US it is 60 Hz.

    Noise gate

    You hear this with Hear it polished, which switches on when you move a control.

    Off

    Turns down whatever is left between phrases (room tone, headphone spill, chair creaks). It only acts well below the singing level, so phrase endings are kept.

    AI clean-up
    Full

    A trained noise-removal network (DeepFilterNet3) cleans the whole take before tuning: fans, traffic, the room, chatter, hiss. It was trained on speech and on its own it erases held notes, so Sudz guards the harmonics of every sung note using its pitch track; keep Protect sung notes on for singing. Lower Strength leaves a little of the room in, which can sound more natural. The first use downloads about 20 MB.

    Vocal style and export

    The mix-engineer stage: clean-up, an even level, a clear tone, tamed esses, width, echo and reverb. Pick a style in the bar above, press Hear it polished, then switch styles while it plays. Each part can be adjusted from the Clean-up, EQ, Dynamics and Effects menus.

    To save the polished vocal as a file, use Export… at the top (WAV or AIFF).

    Split a song into vocal and backing

    Pulls the lead vocal out of a finished song with a trained separation network (HT-Demucs, vocals specialist, MIT licence). Use it to tune or study a vocal from a mix, get an instrumental to sing over, or take a reference vocal for tone matching. It runs in the background in your browser, so you can keep working: roughly 10 to 15 minutes for a 3-minute song.

    1. The model

    It’s 166 MB, so Sudz doesn’t download it for you: get htdemucs_ft_vocals_fp16weights.onnx from huggingface.co/StemSplitio/htdemucs-ft-vocals-onnx (Files tab, the download arrow), then choose it here. Sudz keeps it in this browser for next time.

    Live effects

    The vocal chain runs inside GarageBand as the song plays: low cut, vocal rider, compressor, tone, saturation, de-esser, reverb, doubler, echo and the rest of the style. Tweak while it plays. GarageBand’s automation lanes for Sudz (Reverb amount, Echo amount, Doubler amount, Vocal level) ride on top of these, for a bigger chorus or an echo throw. Noise reduction, de-reverb, tone matching, harmonies and the choir need the whole song at once, so they stay in the browser version’s export.

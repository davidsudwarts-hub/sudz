Installing Sudz (GarageBand plug-in and app)

1. Unzip Sudz-mac.zip (double-click it). You get a folder called Sudz.
2. Open Terminal and run (drag install.command into the Terminal window instead of typing the path, if you like):

   bash ~/Downloads/Sudz/install.command

3. Quit and reopen GarageBand. In your vocal track's plug-ins, remove Sudz and add it again (Audio Units > Sudz).

What goes where:
  Sudz.component -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (the GarageBand plug-in)
  Sudz.app       -> /Applications                          (the full editor on its own)
  AI engine      -> ~/Library/Application Support/Sudz/lib (ONNX Runtime, for fast AI clean-up and song splitting)

The plug-in and app are signed for local use only, which is why they're installed with the command above
rather than by double-clicking. If the app says Apple can't check it, run:
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Sudz.app
